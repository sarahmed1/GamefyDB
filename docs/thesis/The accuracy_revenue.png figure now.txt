  Where SARIMA wins:

  Where Prophet wins:
  - Session volume: by 21 points (54.7% vs 75.7%) — that's a big gap on the most operationally useful forecast

  And the practical blockers for switching:
  - SARIMA can't produce 4-week + 3-month forecasts cleanly — it would need recursive step-by-step prediction which compounds
  errors (same issue as XGBoost)
  - SARIMA doesn't give confidence intervals out of the box in the format Power BI expects — the planning range bands on the
  dashboard would break
  - SARIMA needs a regular time index — the missing days in your data would need imputation first
  - The entire chatbot, pipeline, and Power BI output is wired around Prophet's output format — switching would break a lot
